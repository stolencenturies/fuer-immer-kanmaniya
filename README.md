## 𓂃 The story behind this little universe

Once upon a time, somewhere in this enormous world, two people found each other.

She was our Ravenclaw — thoughtful, clever, gentle, and full of little dreams. The kind of person who could make an ordinary day feel like a page from a beautiful book.

He was our Slytherin — ambitious, determined, a little stubborn, and always planning for the future.

Different houses. Different personalities. One little universe.

And somewhere along the way, they started imagining a life beyond the distance between them.

A life with quiet mornings, warm cafés, rainy streets, old bookstores, and perhaps a little apartment somewhere in Germany.

This website is a collection of the little things that make their story theirs.

## 🗺️ The German Dream

*Ein Traum, den wir eines Tages gemeinsam leben möchten.*

One day, perhaps, there will be no more counting the distance between two cities.

Just the two of us, walking through beautiful German streets, discovering little cafés, visiting bookstores, learning new things, and making memories that don't need to be saved on a screen.

Maybe Munich. Maybe somewhere quieter.

A little home, two cups of coffee, books on the shelves, flowers by the window, and a thousand ordinary days that feel extraordinary because we get to share them.

**Unser kleines Zuhause. Unser großes Abenteuer.**

## 🏰 Two houses, one story

### 💙 Her — The Ravenclaw

*Wit beyond measure is a person's greatest treasure.*

A thoughtful soul, a beautiful mind, and a heart full of dreams.

She reminds him that life isn't only about reaching destinations. Sometimes the most beautiful part is noticing the little things along the way.

### 💚 Him — The Slytherin

*Ambition, patience, and determination.*

A dreamer with plans, a mind that keeps building, and a heart that wants to turn their someday into something real.

He believes that even the longest journeys begin with one small step.

### Together

Not two halves searching for completion, but two people choosing to grow, dream, and build beautiful memories alongside each other.

## 🇩🇪 Ein Brief an unsere Zukunft

*To the two people we will become.*

Dear future us,

I hope we're reading this somewhere in Germany.

Perhaps it's raining outside. Perhaps we're sitting beside a window in a little café, talking about how everything started.

I hope we've travelled, learned, grown, laughed, and collected countless stories.

And I hope we still remember this little website — the letters, the photographs, the dreams, and the two houses that somehow became one story.

Until then, let's keep building our little mosaic, one memory at a time.
